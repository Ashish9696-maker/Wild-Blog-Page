HERE ARE SOME INTERESTING FACTS ABOUT RHINOS:

1- Thick Skin: Rhino skin is incredibly thick, ranging from 1.5 to 5 centimeters (0.6 to 2 inches). Despite its thick appearance, their skin is very sensitive and prone to sunburn, which is why they take mud baths to protect their skin from the sun and parasites.

2- Huge Size: Rhinos are one of the largest land mammals, second only to elephants. The white rhino, for example, can weigh up to 2,300 kg (5,000 lbs).

3- Two Horns, Not One: The majority of rhinos have two horns on their snout. However, the Javan rhino and Sumatran rhino only have one horn. The horns are made of keratin, the same protein found in human hair and nails!

HERE ARE SOME INTERESTING FACTS ABOUT LIONS:



1- Social Animals: Lions are the only cats that live in groups, called prides. A pride can have up to 30 lions, including males, females, and cubs.

2- Powerful Roar: A lion's roar can be heard up to 8 kilometers (5 miles) away, making it one of the loudest roars in the animal kingdom.

3- Top Predators: Lions are apex predators and play a crucial role in maintaining the balance of their ecosystem.
